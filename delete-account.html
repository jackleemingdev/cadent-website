Delete your Cadent account

Last updated: 15 October 2026

You can delete your Cadent account and everything stored with it at any time.

In the app

  • Open Cadent and tap the gear to open Settings.
  • Tap Account, then Delete Account, and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email questions@cadentapp.net from the email address you signed up with, asking for your account to be deleted. We will delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account, your workouts and their routes, your goals, the files stored with your workouts, your feedback messages and your app usage records. Nothing is kept. Our hosting provider's backups are removed after a limited period, and crash reports are removed by our crash reporting service after 90 days.

Workouts in Apple Health or Health Connect on your phone are not affected. Cadent only reads them.
